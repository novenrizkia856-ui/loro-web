Open offers

Each offer is already funded. Lock the exact collateral it asks for and the stablecoin arrives in the same transaction. Repay principal plus the fixed interest by the deadline and the collateral comes back.

Write the terms

You fund the offer now and set every term. Once a borrower accepts, nothing about it can change.

Your offers and loans

What you owe

Repay any time up to and including the deadline second. After it passes, repayment is closed and anyone can open the auction.

Collateral for sale

Loans past their deadline. Anyone can open the auction, and anyone can fill it at the live price, which only falls. The whole payment goes to the lender, less the protocol fee on interest.

Loro contracts have no admin, no oracle and no upgrade path. The chain is the source of truth; this page only reads it.
