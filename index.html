The problem

Two ways to raise cash.
Both cost you.

Loro needs neither.

01

Sell the asset

You raise cash. You give up the position.

Every gain after the sale belongs to someone else. Buying back in costs whatever the market asks that day.

    02

    Borrow offchain

    You keep the asset. You give up your privacy.

    Paperwork, identity checks, and a desk that decides whether you qualify. The terms can move after you sign.

      What Loro is

      Loro is a lending protocol. You lock a Stock Token or ETH. You borrow stablecoin against it. The rate and the term are set on day one. Neither one moves. No one approves you first. No one can change the terms later. Repay by the deadline and the collateral is yours again. Miss the deadline and it goes to auction. That is the whole protocol.

      By design

      Four numbers that never move.

      The life of a loan

      1. Day 0 Collateral locked. Stablecoin out.
      2. Every day after The rate holds. The deadline holds.
      3. Deadline Repaid. The collateral comes back. Not repaid. The collateral is auctioned.
